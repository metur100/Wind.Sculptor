Wind Sculptor – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

Wind Sculptor (the "App") is a game made by Medin Turkes / Certi Development ("we"). This policy explains how the App handles your information. In short: the App does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking. It does not make any network requests while you play.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, scores, daily challenge streak, collection) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, reduced motion) are saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and we cannot access it.

You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the game, or by uninstalling the App.

Permissions

The App only uses the device's vibration / haptic engine for feedback (which can be turned off in the game's settings). It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Third parties

We do not sell, trade or share any information with third parties, because we do not collect any.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
