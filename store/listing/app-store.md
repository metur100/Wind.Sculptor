**Description** (max 4000)
```
Shape the wind. Sculpt the world.

Wind Sculptor is a calm, satisfying puzzle game where you never touch a single particle. Swipe across the screen to create gusts of wind and blow drifting sand, autumn leaves, snowflakes, confetti, fireflies and bubbles into a target shape before the timer runs out.

Loose particles become wind trails – and wind trails become a finished sculpture.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe to create wind. Faster swipes blow harder.
• Guide the particles into the glowing outline.
• Fill the shape to earn up to three stars.
• Run out of time? Keep sculpting, retry, or ask for a hint.

FEATURES
• 30 handcrafted levels across 6 dreamy worlds: Sand Dunes, Autumn Park, Winter Peaks, Festival, Night Forest and Deep Ocean
• 6 materials, each with its own physics – heavy sand, fluttering leaves, floating snow, spinning confetti, wandering fireflies and rising bubbles
• New challenges as you go: crosswinds, fans, walls, icy ponds, drains, limited wind energy, moving targets and two sculptures at once
• A new Daily Challenge every day – the same puzzle for everyone, with a streak to keep
• Collect a sculpture card for every shape you complete
• Interactive tutorial that teaches you in under a minute
• Relaxing generative music, gentle sound effects and haptic feedback
• Free hints – nothing in the game is locked behind ads or purchases

PLAY ANYWHERE
• Works completely offline
• No account, no sign-up, no data collected
• Accessible design: large touch targets, colour-independent star ratings and a reduced-motion setting

Take a breath, draw a gust and watch the wind do the rest.
```
